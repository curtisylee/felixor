Adoption is where AI programs quietly die.

The budget gets approved. The platform team ships the endpoints. A model is standing by behind an internal gateway, and on paper the company has AI. Six months later usage sits in the single digits and nobody can say exactly what went wrong.

It is almost never the model. It is everything wrapped around the model: who it was built for, what it actually replaces, whether it fits the way the work already moves, and whether anyone was given a reason to open it twice.

That layer is a product problem. It is the part most AI programs treat as a rollout email.

How we work

Find the surface worth building.

We start where AI changes a workflow, not where it demos well. Interviews with the people doing the work, a map of where time and judgment actually go, and a shortlist of the two or three bets that survive contact with real users.

You end with a ranked set of opportunities and an honest read on what each one costs to ship.

Build the product, not the prototype.

Production interfaces, evaluation harnesses, guardrails, retrieval that holds up under real documents, and the unglamorous integration work that decides whether any of it survives a security review.

Built inside your stack, in your repositories, with your engineers in the room so the knowledge stays after we leave.

Earn the adoption.

Rollout design, enablement that respects people's time, internal champions with a real mandate, and instrumentation that shows whether the thing stuck or quietly got abandoned.

Most programs stop before this step. It is the only part that compounds.

Hyperscaler product craft, pointed at your AI program.

Felixor was founded by Curtis Lee, formerly a Microsoft executive who led product for Azure Experiences and Ecosystems: the user-facing surface of Azure and the AI initiatives built on top of it.

That job was this problem at a different scale. Capability was never scarce. What people chose to adopt, return to, and build their week around was. The difference between the two was almost always product work that nobody had budgeted for.

Felixor exists to do that work for companies that have the models and are still waiting for the results.
